## Source work on 2026-09-24

- The shared water recipe now computes each wave's projected phase footprint
  before `Frac` and `Cosine`, smoothly fades wavelengths across 0.25–0.5
  cycles/pixel, and transfers removed slope variance into perceptual
  roughness. Both saved water materials were subsequently inspected through
  Unreal MCP; the recipe alone is not asset evidence.
- The course actor now pitches the original sculling-oar blade through the
  boat-relative waterline during fresh drive presentation and feathers it
  during recovery or stale input. It tracks rendered blade crossings with one
  event per entry or exit, suppresses repeat and stale events, and re-arms on
  reconnect without backfilling. A later source update adds a fixed pool of
  non-colliding, world-anchored planes for short-lived hull and blade effects.
  Hull samples require short, fresh presented movement and never bridge a
  skipped distance, reconnect, or route switch. Blade crossings spawn one
  ripple each; reduced motion clears the pool. The cooked-in translucent
  interaction material has an Editor Python recipe and was saved as a

- Han source preflight now follows package references transitively from the
  runtime map through materials to texture packages. The external Han cook

  checks the missing and present cases.

texture group, streaming enabled, and green-channel flip off. A separate
`M_CourseWater_DetailTrial` material samples it with world-anchored, ambient
motion UVs. The sample's projected feature footprint (64 features per UV
repeat, a provisional 16 texel feature scale) fades its normal slope at
distance; its XY slope is mixed at a trial strength of 0.16 with the existing
procedural normal. The material recompiled and saved clean, and its asset
dependency lists the imported texture. Trial-only, source, and texture-path
metadata were saved on the material. The 256 px material thumbnail is too
small to judge the chase view. The optional second argument to
`Scripts/build_water_material.py` now expresses the one-sample trial graph;
that source option passed Python syntax compilation but has not been run inside
the Editor. A saved `MI_CourseWater_BaselineTrial` instance matches the
course's runtime `DeepColor=(0.03, 0.20, 0.35)` for controlled A/B previews.

suitability remain unverified. No trial bitmap is in
a Han package or cooked Shipping build; the owner has not selected a winner.

variant shows broad bright patches in the middle distance as well as near-field

the Editor duplicated it as `/Game/Water/Trial/M_CourseWater_SubtleTrial` and
set `DetailStrengthTrial=0.06`. The duplicate recompiled, saved clean, and its
only reported asset dependency is the Water 002 trial normal. Trial-only and
source metadata record the strength, creator, page, license, and source hash.
It has been compared in a paused Editor view below, but not in a packaged chase
view or measured on Metal.
